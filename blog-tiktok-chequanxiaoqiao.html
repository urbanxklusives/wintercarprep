What to do if the ABS warning light is on?

What to take from it

  • Your seatbelt can push your car. Pull the belt all the way out, close the door, loop it over your shoulder — and even a small person can push a broken-down car with real leverage.
  • Check the bottom of the belt webbing. Mud, sand, or moldy spots down low mean the car was flooded. Walk away from that used car.
  • Compare the dates. The date tag on the seatbelt should match the car's build date. A mismatch can mean the airbags went off in a major crash.
  • The buckle tongue breaks glass. Trapped inside? The metal edge of the seatbelt buckle can smash the corner of a side window to get you out.
  • An ABS light is not a suggestion. Her second video covers what the ABS warning light means and why you should not ignore it — especially before winter driving.
