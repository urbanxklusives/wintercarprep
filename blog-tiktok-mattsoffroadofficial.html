Even the Rescuers Get Stuck: Matt's Off-Road Recovery in the Snow

'We'll get em out!' — the recovery crew that pulls RVs out of mud and SUVs out of snow, one winch at a time.

Advertisement

Matt Wetzel and his crew run a real recovery business out of southern Utah, near Zion National Park — and their TikTok bio is the whole promise: "We'll get em out!" RVs buried in mud, trucks on cliff edges, SUVs stuck in snow at night: this is the crew that gets the call when nobody else can do it. Their TikTok has 82,800 followers and more than 400 videos of real recoveries, filmed the way they actually happen — messy, muddy, and sometimes funny. The best proof they are the real deal? Their own wrecker gets stuck too, and they film that as well.

What to take from it

  • Even the pros get stuck. Recovery is a skill, not magic — and the crew's worst-stuck moments teach more than their easy wins.
  • The right rope matters. Matt's crew uses kinetic recovery ropes that stretch and snap a stuck vehicle free — far safer and more effective than a stiff chain or a random tow strap.
  • Know when to call for help. If you are buried to the axles, on a slope, or near water, stop digging and call someone with the right rig. Pride is cheaper than a transmission.
