What this site is

Winter Car Prep is a small guide site for drivers who face real winters. Every article answers one question in plain language: what to do, what it costs, and what you can skip.

How the site makes money

The site is built to earn through advertising and affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, it earns from qualifying purchases made through links on the site, at no extra cost to the reader. Ad placements are marked as advertisements.

A note on accuracy

Guides are written from general automotive knowledge and public safety guidance. Prices are typical ranges in Canada and the US and change over time. Always confirm fitment, specs and prices for your own vehicle before buying parts.
