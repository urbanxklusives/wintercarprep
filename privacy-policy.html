Privacy Policy

What this site collects, and what it doesn't — in plain language.

Advertisement

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Winter Car Prep is a content website about winter car care. This page explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you visit, how it is used, and what choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at contact@wintercarprep.com and we will answer in plain words.

Who we are

Winter Car Prep (wintercarprep.com) is an independent guide site run from Canada. There is no company behind it collecting your data for resale — the site makes money from advertising and affiliate links, as described below. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anyone.

What information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, home address, phone number or payment information. You can read every guide and use every tool on this site without telling us who you are.

Like most websites, limited information is collected automatically when you visit:

  • Server and hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may briefly log standard technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as part of running the service. We do not see or store these logs ourselves.
  • Emails you send us: if you email contact@wintercarprep.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not add it to any list or share it with third parties.
  • Information you type into the tools: the "Will My Car Start Tomorrow?" tool asks for a town name and your battery's and car's ages. That information is processed in your browser and sent to the Open-Meteo weather service (see below). We do not store it, and we have no way to see it. The Winter Kit Builder and the tire pressure calculator run entirely in your browser — nothing you enter leaves your device.

The "Will My Car Start Tomorrow?" tool

This tool needs tomorrow's overnight low for your town, so it sends the place name you type to Open-Meteo's free geocoding service to find the location, then requests a weather forecast for those coordinates. That is the only data the tool sends anywhere. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request. If you would rather not send a place name, the tool also offers a manual option: check any weather app for your town's overnight low and type the number in yourself.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. This site itself sets no cookies of its own. Cookies on this site come from third parties:

  • Analytics — Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and measure how pages are used (see Analytics below).
  • Advertising partners (see below) may set cookies to serve relevant ads and to measure how ads perform.
  • Embedded video players — YouTube videos and TikTok embeds on our pages — may set cookies when the video loads, even if you do not press play.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies will not stop you reading the site, though some embedded videos may behave differently.

Advertising

This site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense and may in future use other advertising networks. Google and its partners use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. This is how the site pays for itself.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings, and through aboutads.info or youradchoices.ca for Canadian visitors. Opting out means you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people read and how the site is used. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data — pages visited, approximate location (city level), device and browser type — and does not identify you personally. We do not combine this data with anything else. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site include embedded content from YouTube and TikTok, and links to other websites. Embedded content behaves as if you visited the other website directly: it may collect data about you, use cookies, and track your interaction with the video. Those services have their own privacy policies — Google's privacy policy (YouTube) and TikTok's privacy policy — and this policy does not cover them.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, this site earns from qualifying purchases made through those links, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links do not change the price you pay, and they do not give us any information about you — the retailer knows only that the visit came from this site.

Sponsored placements

Occasionally a business pays for a labelled placement on this site. Sponsored placements are always marked "Sponsored" and are kept separate from the guides themselves. Sponsors do not influence what the guides say, and they receive no information about our readers.

How long we keep information

We keep emails you send us for as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of corrections and requests — normally no longer than one year, unless the law requires longer. We keep nothing else: the tools store nothing, and the site keeps no visitor database.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts the connection between your browser and the site. Email is not encrypted, so please do not send sensitive personal information by email — a question about a guide never needs your address, licence plate or financial details.

Your rights

If you have emailed us, you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — write to contact@wintercarprep.com and we will respond within 30 days. This site is operated from Canada, and Canadian privacy law (including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, PIPEDA) applies to the limited personal information we handle.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has contacted us, email contact@wintercarprep.com and we will delete the message.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time — for example, if we add analytics or a new advertising partner. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be noted here in plain language.

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@wintercarprep.com.
