What to take from it

  • Tires age out, not just wear out. Even with tread left, old rubber hardens and grips less — especially in the cold. Age matters as much as wear.
  • Know your tires before winter. Worn or aged tires are the difference between stopping and sliding. Check them in fall, not after the first storm.
  • Small jobs are easy jobs. Chaya's wiper-blade lesson is the same idea: most blades use a simple hook, take a few minutes, and the instructions are right on the package. Lift the arm, lay a towel down first so a snapped-back arm cannot crack the glass.
