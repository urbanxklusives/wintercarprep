The Full Winter Car Checklist

Every job, in the order to do them. Print it, stick it on the fridge, check it off.

Advertisement

October — before the cold

  • ☐ Book the tire swap (beat the rush)
  • ☐ Test the battery (free at most parts stores)
  • ☐ Check antifreeze / coolant level and strength
  • ☐ Top up winter windshield washer fluid
  • ☐ Replace wiper blades if they streak
  • ☐ Book undercoating

November — first snow ready

  • ☐ Winter tires on, torqued, pressures set
  • ☐ Emergency kit packed and checked
  • ☐ Scraper + brush in the car (not the trunk, buried)
  • ☐ Locks and door seals treated (silicone spray stops freezing)
  • ☐ Lights checked — all of them, including brake lights

All winter — every 2–3 weeks

  • ☐ Underbody wash
  • ☐ Tire pressures (cold drops them ~1 PSI per 5°C)
  • ☐ Washer fluid topped up
  • ☐ Fuel tank kept above half (less condensation, more range if stuck)

Spring — undo winter

  • ☐ Swap back to summers, inspect winters for wear
  • ☐ Full underbody wash + rust inspection
  • ☐ Check brakes — salt and grit wear pads faster
