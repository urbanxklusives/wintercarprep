The Full Winter Car Checklist

Every job, in the order to do them. Print it, stick it on the fridge, check it off.

Advertisement

Winter car prep is not one big job — it is a dozen small ones, and the trick is doing them in the right order, before the cold makes them hard. This checklist puts every job in sequence: October prep, November readiness, the all-winter routine, and the spring undo. Work through it once in the fall and you will not be the person queueing at the tire shop in the first snowstorm.

How to use this checklist

Print the page, or work through it on your phone in the driveway. Most October jobs take under an hour each, and several are free — the battery test at a parts store, the light check, the washer fluid top-up. The two jobs worth booking early are the tire swap and the undercoating: both get busy the week the first snow is forecast. If you only have one weekend, do the tires, the battery test and the emergency kit first — those three cover the breakdowns that actually strand people.

October — before the cold

  • ☐ Book the tire swap (beat the rush)
  • ☐ Test the battery (free at most parts stores)
  • ☐ Check antifreeze / coolant level and strength
  • ☐ Top up winter windshield washer fluid
  • ☐ Replace wiper blades if they streak
  • ☐ Book undercoating

November — first snow ready

  • ☐ Winter tires on, torqued, pressures set
  • ☐ Emergency kit packed and checked (build your personal kit list)
  • ☐ Scraper + brush in the car (not the trunk, buried)
  • ☐ Locks and door seals treated (silicone spray stops freezing)
  • ☐ Lights checked — all of them, including brake lights

All winter — every 2–3 weeks

  • ☐ Underbody wash
  • ☐ Tire pressures (cold drops them ~1 PSI per 5°C — check yours with the calculator)
  • ☐ Washer fluid topped up
  • ☐ Fuel tank kept above half (less condensation, more range if stuck)

Spring — undo winter

  • ☐ Swap back to summers, inspect winters for wear
  • ☐ Full underbody wash + rust inspection
  • ☐ Check brakes — salt and grit wear pads faster
