What to take from it

  • Check before you pay, not after. The Master's five checks happen before money changes hands — once you own the problem, it is yours.
  • Winter exposes every weakness. A used car that seems fine in fall can fail on the first -20°C morning. Check the battery, tires, and coolant before winter, not during it.
  • Learn one thing a day. That is the Master's whole method — a small car lesson every day adds up to a driver who cannot be fooled.
